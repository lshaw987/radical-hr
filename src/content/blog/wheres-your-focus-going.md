Control what you can. Let go of what you can't. Sounds simple, right?

And yet, so much energy gets wasted worrying about things completely outside our control - what others think, global events, the latest policy change, or whether it's going to rain on Saturday.

Here's a better way to think about it:

🟢 **Circle of Control** - The things you have direct power over: your decisions, your actions, your mindset. This is where real progress happens.

🔵 **Circle of Influence** - The things you can impact indirectly: team culture, relationships, project outcomes. Influence is built through trust and action.

🟣 **Circle of Concern** - The things you have zero control over: the economy, other people's opinions, global trends. The more time you spend here, the more drained you feel.

A small shift in focus can change everything. What if, instead of getting caught in the 'concern' spiral, you put your energy where it actually makes a difference?

💡 Here's a challenge: Grab a piece of paper, draw three circles, and start placing what's on your mind into the right categories. The perspective shift is game-changing. (Need a template? DM me, and I'll send it over!)

People who actively use this framework find they:

✔️ Get more done - by channeling effort into what actually moves the needle.

✔️ Feel less stressed - because they're not burning energy on what they can't change.

✔️ Make better decisions - by focusing where their actions make an impact.

So, where's your energy going today? Is it helping you move forward - or keeping you stuck?
